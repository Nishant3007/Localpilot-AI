**Title:** LocalPilot AI: A Local-First Business Copilot for Small Restaurants

**Tags:** devchallenge, hf26challenge, opensource, ai

## What I Built
LocalPilot AI is a local-first business copilot that helps restaurant owners track sales, understand estimated ingredient contribution, manage menu prices, and generate marketing copy. It targets small businesses that need affordable tools and control over their data. The Super Zaika workspace contains illustrative data for the demo.

## Why Does Open Innovation Matter?
Running inference through Ollama keeps restaurant data on a machine the owner controls and avoids mandatory per-token proprietary API fees. The model can be swapped without rewriting business logic. Note that compute, electricity, hosting and maintenance still cost money.
